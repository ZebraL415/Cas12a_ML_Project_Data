## Evidence

**Facts confirmed from files**

- The paper defines a five-state A/C/T/G/`-` one-hot encoding and states that `-` may occur in target or crRNA.
- The paper defines 22 original templates with eight variants per group and documents the 96-well plate-control normalization method.
- Table S3 has 10,634 rows, including 740 target representations containing `-`.
- The public official repository contains no generator for `target_at_guide` or `guide_target_hamming_dist`.

**Preliminary judgments from structure and sequence**

- Consecutive nine-row Table S2 groups follow the paper order, with length patterns supporting members 8/9 as insertion/deletion templates.
- Table S3 source candidates were generated by exact/IUPAC-compatible window search; this is sequence evidence, not an author experimental key.

## Data Quality Checks

- All 10,634 alignments have 25-position guides and aligned targets; all 740 gaps are preserved.
- Gap ungapped-length distribution: 4 at 17 nt, 2 at 18 nt, 47 at 23 nt, and 687 at 24 nt.
- Raw Hamming agrees in 10,446 rows and disagrees in 188 rows.
- Source-mapping candidates exist for 10,633 rows: 7,421 high, 3,146 medium, and 67 review.
